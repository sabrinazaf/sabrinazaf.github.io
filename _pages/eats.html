01 · where

Everywhere I've ranked

Every circle is a city. Size is how many places I've ranked there; colour is how well they did, the average rank percentile of my picks (cities with three or more). Dashed rings are my want-to-try list. Click a circle for the highlights.

City-level only: the Beli export has no street addresses, so 200-odd New York places share one circle. Scroll-zoom turns on after you click the map.

02 · home turf

Where I eat, and where I want to

Solid bars are places I've ranked; dashed bars are bookmarked places I haven't tried yet.

03 · the log

When I add things

New entries per month, stacked by category. A place is added when I rank it, so this is a log of when I rated things, which is close to, but not exactly, when I ate there.

04 · the model

What lines up with where I rank a place?

Beli ranks each category in order, so every category has its own #1. I turned each rank into a percentile score (100 = my favourite, 0 = my least favourite) and asked what lines up with it. It's exploratory: about 430 data points, observational, nothing causal. Dots are effects in percentile points with 95% confidence intervals; fuchsia means the interval clears zero.
